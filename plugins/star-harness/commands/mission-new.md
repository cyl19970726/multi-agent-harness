Use `$ARGUMENTS` as the objective sketch and follow
`[[orchestrate-mission-waves]]`. Select the project explicitly, write durable
Mission Markdown, append the initial Mission Log judgment, and report the exact
returned ids. Do not create or mutate a Wave: Wave writes are retired and
historical Wave reads are Legacy-only. Do not start providers or sensitive
external actions without the applicable approval.
