# Team War Room Works expected-design prompt

The current browser captures are edit targets. Preserve Firm shell,
identity, portraits, Works semantics, status vocabulary, and context modules.
Do not invent data or restore Assignment Messages.

The approved direction makes Shared Works the visual center, adds a compact
truthful capacity summary, reduces filter and card chrome, and keeps the right
rail visually secondary. Tablet and mobile preserve workflow priority rather
than shrinking desktop: real Work appears in the first viewport and the
composer/context controls collapse until requested.

Exact normalized expected sizes are 1440×1000, 900×1180, and 390×844. The
generated concept originals are retained beside them; the normalized images
only apply the minimal proportional resize/crop needed for exact comparison.

Exclude task graphs, Assignment Messages, charts, gradients, mascot overlays,
raw JSON, unobservable analytics, and fake progress.
